Hi — I'm Mark Mwenda, a digital marketer and content strategist based in Nakuru, Kenya. I started DigiCreativ because I kept seeing great local businesses with weak or non-existent online presence — and I knew I could fix that.


I help businesses build their brand, manage their social media, and create content that actually connects with their audience. My approach is honest, hands-on, and results-focused — I work directly with every client, no middlemen.
